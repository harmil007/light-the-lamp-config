Privacy Policy for Find the Legends: Word Search
Last Updated: August 12, 2026

OcenSoft ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Find the Legends: Word Search mobile application. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the game.

1. Information We Collect
Find the Legends: Word Search does not require a user account, registration, login, or social media connection. We do not directly collect or store personally identifiable information (PII) such as:

Your name
Your email address
Your phone number
Your physical address
All of your gameplay progress (unlocked levels, discovered historical legends cards, stars balance, and system settings) is stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertisements
To keep the game free to play, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may automatically collect certain device-specific information to serve and personalize ads, including:

Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / Apple ID for Advertisers)
Device model and operating system version
Approximate IP-based location
App diagnostics and usage logs
This data collection is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy, which you can read at: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Local Storage (MMKV)
The application utilizes secure local storage (MMKV) to save your completed stages, total stars, unlocked custom themes, sound/music configurations, and haptic preferences. This data remains on your physical device, is never sent to our servers, and is deleted if you uninstall the application.

4. Internet Connection & Offline Mode
Most features of Find the Legends: Word Search are available fully offline. An internet connection is only utilized to request advertisements from Google AdMob or to load store updates.

5. Children's Privacy
Our game is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
